    <p># Waste to Taste

## Turning Food Waste into Delicious Innovation

Welcome to **Waste to Taste**, where we believe that every ingredient has value. Our mission is to reduce food waste by transforming everyday leftovers into delicious, sustainable, and creative food products.

### Our Vision

To create a greener future by giving food a second life. We aim to inspire people to reduce waste, protect the environment, and enjoy nutritious products made from ingredients that are often thrown away.

### Our Products

**🥔 Potato Peel Chips**
Crispy, flavorful chips made from fresh potato peels. Instead of discarding the peels, we turn them into a tasty and healthy snack packed with natural goodness.

**🍞 Roti Revival Cake**
A soft and delicious cake prepared from leftover rotis. This innovative recipe reduces food waste while creating a unique dessert that everyone can enjoy.

**🍉 Watermelon Seed Candies**
Sweet, crunchy candies made from nutritious watermelon seeds. We transform seeds that are usually discarded into a delightful treat rich in nutrients.

### Why Choose Waste to Taste?

* Reduces food waste and promotes sustainability.
* Creates delicious products from overlooked ingredients.
* Supports eco-friendly and responsible food practices.
* Encourages creative solutions for a cleaner planet.

### Our Mission

Every year, tons of edible food are wasted. At Waste to Taste, we are committed to changing this by converting food scraps and leftovers into innovative products that are both tasty and environmentally friendly. Every bite helps reduce waste and supports a more sustainable future.

### Join Us

Together, we can make a difference. Choose smarter, waste less, and enjoy food that gives ingredients a second chance. Let's turn waste into taste—one bite at a time!
